Good to know

Partnership FAQ

How much does a partnership cost?
It depends on the format and length of the campaign. Student discounts and free perks can often be listed at no cost. Email us your goal and we'll share options and pricing.
Do partners get access to student data?
No. ClassTrack never sells student personal data and never shares it with partners for their own marketing. You receive aggregated campaign results such as views and clicks.
Is sponsored content labelled?
Yes. Every paid placement in the app or on the blog is clearly marked as sponsored or partner content.
I want to write an unpaid guest post instead.
Great — see our Write for us page for topics and guidelines.
How fast will you reply?
We usually reply within 1–2 working days.
