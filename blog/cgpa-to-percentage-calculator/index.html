Quick answer

To convert CGPA to percentage, multiply your CGPA by 9.5 (the most common formula, used by CBSE and many Indian universities): Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. Example: 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%. To go the other way, divide: CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5. Use the calculator below — it converts both ways as you type, and you can change the multiplier to match your university.

The CGPA to percentage formula

Most Indian universities and boards convert on a 10-point scale using a single multiplier:

calculate

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

This ×9.5 rule was popularised by the CBSE and is now the default at many institutions. To reverse it, simply divide: CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5.

Worked example

Suppose your CGPA is 8.5 and your university uses the ×9.5 formula:

  • CGPA: 8.5
  • Multiplier: 9.5
  • Calculation: 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%

Going the other way, a percentage of 80.75 ÷ 9.5 = 8.5 CGPA. The calculator does this rounding for you automatically.

SGPA, CGPA and percentage — what's the difference?

  • SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is your average for a single semester.
  • CGPA (Cumulative GPA) is the average across all completed semesters.
  • Percentage is CGPA (or SGPA) converted using your university's multiplier.

To turn SGPA into a percentage, use the same formula: SGPA × 9.5. To find CGPA from multiple SGPAs, average them (weighted by credits if your university requires it), then convert.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?
Multiply your CGPA by your university's multiplier. The most common formula is Percentage = CGPA × 9.5, used by CBSE and many Indian universities. For example, a CGPA of 8.5 equals 8.5 × 9.5 = 80.75%. Always check your own university's rule, as some use a different multiplier or formula.
What is the CGPA to percentage formula?
The standard formula is Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. Some universities use CGPA × 10, and a few use their own formula such as (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. Use the multiplier printed in your university's grading rules.
How do I convert percentage to CGPA?
Divide your percentage by the same multiplier. With the ×9.5 formula, CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5. For example, 80.75% ÷ 9.5 = 8.5 CGPA. The calculator on this page converts both ways automatically.
Is CGPA to percentage always ×9.5?
No. ×9.5 is the most common (CBSE and many Indian universities), but multipliers vary by institution. Some use ×10 or a custom formula. Change the multiplier in the calculator to match your university before relying on the result.
What is a good CGPA?
On a 10-point scale, a CGPA of 7.5+ is generally considered good and 8.5+ is excellent. What counts as good also depends on your goals — see our guide on what a good GPA means for jobs, scholarships and grad school.
