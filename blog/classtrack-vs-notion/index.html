ClassTrack vs Notion for Students

schedule 8 min read update Updated August 2026 verified By Prince Kumar

Quick answer

Notion is a flexible, build-it-yourself workspace; ClassTrack is a purpose-built student app that works out of the box. If you want to tinker and build your own dashboards, Notion is powerful. If you just want attendance, a bunk calculator, timetable, exams and GPA tracked automatically, ClassTrack wins on time saved — it does the things Notion can't (like attendance %) with zero setup. Many students use both.

Quick verdict

Choose ClassTrack if you want student-specific tracking (attendance %, bunk calculator, GPA, exam countdowns) with no setup. Choose Notion if you love customising and want one flexible workspace for notes, wikis and projects. They're not really rivals — Notion is a blank canvas, ClassTrack is a finished tool.

When Notion is the better choice

  • You enjoy building and customising your own system.
  • You want one workspace for notes, wikis, reading lists and group projects.
  • You don't need attendance percentages or a bunk calculator.

When ClassTrack is the better choice

  • You want attendance, GPA, timetable and exam tracking that just works.
  • You care about the bunk calculator — Notion can't replicate it easily.
  • You'd rather spend time studying than maintaining a template.

Using both together

A common setup: ClassTrack for classes, attendance, deadlines and GPA; Notion for long-form notes, project wikis and reading. Let each do what it's best at instead of forcing one to do everything.

Key takeaways

  • Many students use ClassTrack for tracking and Notion for notes.

Frequently asked questions

Is ClassTrack or Notion better for students?
It depends on what you want. ClassTrack is better for student-specific tracking — attendance %, bunk calculator, timetable, exams and GPA — with no setup. Notion is better for flexible notes, wikis and custom dashboards. Many students use both.
Can Notion track attendance like ClassTrack?
Not really. You can build a manual database in Notion, but it won't calculate your live attendance percentage or tell you how many classes you can safely skip. ClassTrack does both automatically with a built-in bunk calculator.
Is ClassTrack a Notion alternative?
For the student-tracking side, yes — ClassTrack replaces the attendance, timetable, GPA and exam tracking you'd otherwise build in Notion, and it works instantly. For long-form notes and wikis, Notion is still stronger.
Is ClassTrack free like Notion?
ClassTrack's core is free forever, with optional Pro from about $1.25/month for unlimited AI. Notion has a free personal plan with paid upgrades. Both let you start without paying.
Can I use ClassTrack and Notion together?
Yes, and many students do: ClassTrack for classes, attendance, deadlines and GPA; Notion for notes, project wikis and reading lists. Each handles what it does best.
