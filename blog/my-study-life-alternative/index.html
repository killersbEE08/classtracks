Why students look for an alternative

My Study Life is a solid timetable-and-task planner, but students often outgrow it when they need more than scheduling — especially attendance tracking (with the all-important percentage and bunk math), GPA, and faster setup. If you've been copying your timetable in by hand, that's usually the tipping point.

What to look for in an alternative

  • Attendance % + bunk calculator — the biggest gap in most planners.
  • GPA & grade tracking on your scale.
  • Fast timetable setup — ideally build it from a photo instead of typing.
  • Exam countdowns and reminders.
  • Cloud sync and a home-screen widget.
