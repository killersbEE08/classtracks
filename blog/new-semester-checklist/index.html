Frequently asked questions

How should I set up for a new semester?
In the first week, add your full timetable, set an attendance target, enter all known deadlines and exam dates, set a GPA goal, and block weekly study sessions. Getting this done early prevents the mid-term scramble.
What should be on a back-to-school checklist for college?
Timetable, attendance target, deadlines and exam dates, a GPA goal, weekly study blocks, and reminders switched on. Admin like reading lists, log-ins and office hours is worth noting in the quiet first week too.
How do I build my timetable quickly?
Instead of typing recurring events, snap a photo of your printed or PDF timetable and let ClassTrack's AI assistant build it, then review and confirm. It takes minutes rather than an evening.
When should I start tracking attendance?
From your very first class. Building a buffer above the 75% requirement early is far easier than recovering a shortfall later in the term.
What's a good GPA goal to set?
It depends on your goals — scholarships and grad school expect higher. A 3.0+ on a 4.0 scale or 7.5+ on a 10-point scale is a solid general target. See our guide on what a good GPA is.
