ClassTrack vs Google Calendar for Students

schedule 7 min read update Updated August 2026 verified By Prince Kumar

Quick answer

Google Calendar is a great general calendar, but it doesn't understand student life — no attendance %, no bunk calculator, no GPA. ClassTrack is built for it: it tracks attendance, exams and grades, and can build your timetable from a photo. Best of both: keep ClassTrack for student tracking and sync your timetable to Google Calendar for a unified view.

Quick verdict

If all you need is time-blocked events and reminders, Google Calendar is fine. But it can't tell you your attendance percentage, how many classes you can skip, or your GPA. ClassTrack handles the student-specific parts and still plays nicely with Google Calendar via sync.

When Google Calendar is enough

  • You only need class times and reminders, nothing academic-specific.
  • You already live in Google Calendar for everything else.
  • You don't care about attendance percentages or GPA.

When ClassTrack is better

  • You need to protect your attendance and want a bunk calculator.
  • You want exam countdowns, GPA and a focus timer in the same place.
  • You'd rather snap a photo of your timetable than type recurring events.

Using both together

You don't have to choose. Set up your classes in ClassTrack, then add your timetable to Google Calendar so your academic schedule sits next to your personal events — while attendance, exams and GPA stay tracked in ClassTrack.

Key takeaways

  • Google Calendar handles events and reminders but knows nothing about attendance or GPA.
  • ClassTrack is built for student life: attendance, bunk calculator, exams, GPA.
  • ClassTrack can build your timetable from a photo; Google Calendar can't.
  • Best setup: track in ClassTrack, sync the timetable to Google Calendar.

Frequently asked questions

Is ClassTrack better than Google Calendar for students?
For student-specific needs, yes: ClassTrack tracks attendance percentage, has a bunk calculator, exam countdowns and GPA — none of which Google Calendar offers. Google Calendar remains great for general events and sharing, and the two can sync.
Can Google Calendar track my attendance?
No. Google Calendar shows events and reminders but doesn't calculate attendance percentages or tell you how many classes you can miss. ClassTrack does both with a built-in bunk calculator.
Can I sync ClassTrack with Google Calendar?
Yes. You can add your ClassTrack timetable to Google Calendar so your classes appear alongside personal events. See our guide on adding your timetable to Google Calendar.
Do I need a separate app if I use Google Calendar?
If you want attendance tracking, a bunk calculator, GPA or exam countdowns, yes — Google Calendar doesn't do these. ClassTrack covers them and still syncs your schedule to Google Calendar.
Is ClassTrack free like Google Calendar?
ClassTrack's core features are free forever, with an optional Pro plan from about $1.25/month for unlimited AI. Google Calendar is free. You can use both at no cost.
