Quick answer

To convert SGPA to CGPA, multiply each semester's SGPA by its credits, add them up, and divide by the total credits: CGPA = Σ(SGPA × credits) ÷ Σ(credits). The SGPA to CGPA formula

CGPA is the credit-weighted average of your semester SGPAs:

calculate

CGPA = (SGPA₁×C₁ + SGPA₂×C₂ + … + SGPAₙ×Cₙ) ÷ (C₁ + C₂ + … + Cₙ)

Here C is the number of credits in each semester. When every semester has the same credits, the formula simplifies to the plain average:

functions

CGPA = (SGPA₁ + SGPA₂ + … + SGPAₙ) ÷ n

Worked example

Suppose you have completed four semesters, each worth the same credits:

SemesterSGPACredits
Sem 18.220
Sem 28.620
Sem 37.922
Sem 48.818

Grade points = (8.2×20) + (8.6×20) + (7.9×22) + (8.8×18) = 164 + 172 + 173.8 + 158.4 = 668.2. Total credits = 80. So CGPA = 668.2 ÷ 80 = 8.35. Converting with the ×9.5 rule: 8.35 × 9.5 = 79.3%.

SGPA vs CGPA — what's the difference?

  • SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is your grade average for a single semester.
  • CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) combines all completed semesters into one figure.
  • Percentage is either value converted with your university's multiplier (commonly ×9.5).

SGPA / CGPA to percentage

To turn either into a percentage, multiply by your university's factor — most often 9.5:

percent

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 (or SGPA × 9.5)

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate CGPA from SGPA?
Multiply each semester's SGPA by its credits, add up all those products, then divide by the total credits: CGPA = Σ(SGPA × credits) ÷ Σ(credits). If every semester carries equal credits, your CGPA is simply the average of your SGPAs.
What is the SGPA to CGPA formula?
The credit-weighted formula is CGPA = (SGPA₁×C₁ + SGPA₂×C₂ + …) ÷ (C₁ + C₂ + …), where C is the credits in each semester. With equal credits it reduces to CGPA = (SGPA₁ + … + SGPAₙ) ÷ n.
Is CGPA just the average of SGPA?
Only when every semester has the same number of credits. If credits differ, weight each SGPA by its credits so heavier semesters count more. Many universities set equal credits per semester, which makes it a simple average.
How do I convert SGPA or CGPA to percentage?
Use your university's multiplier. The most common formula is Percentage = CGPA × 9.5 (CBSE and many Indian universities), and the same multiplier works for SGPA. Confirm the exact formula in your university's grading rules.
