Free software, cheaper music and money off laptops — just for being a student. Here are 10 deals worth claiming, what each one gives you, who qualifies and the official link to sign up.

Good to know

Student discounts FAQ

What are the best student discounts in 2026?
As of 18 October 2026: the GitHub Student Developer Pack, Office 365 Education, the Notion Education Plan, Figma for Education, JetBrains and Autodesk Education (all free), plus Adobe Creative Cloud Pro (70%+ off the first year in the US), Apple education pricing, Spotify Premium Student and YouTube Premium Student. The ClassTrack app lists more, matched to your country.
Which student discounts are completely free?
Six on this list cost nothing for eligible students: GitHub Student Developer Pack, Office 365 Education, Notion Education Plan, Figma for Education, JetBrains educational licenses and Autodesk Education. Adobe, Apple, Spotify and YouTube give reduced prices instead.
How do I prove I'm a student?
Most brands ask for your school or university email. Some use a verification service like SheerID (Spotify uses it), or accept a student ID, enrollment letter or ISIC card. Expect to re-verify about once a year.
Do these discounts work in India and other countries?
The free software offers (GitHub, Microsoft, Notion, Figma, JetBrains, Autodesk) work for eligible students worldwide. Adobe, Apple, Spotify and YouTube student prices exist in many countries — Spotify Premium Student is ₹69/month in India — but prices and availability change by country, so check the official site for your region.
Where can I find more student discounts?
In the free ClassTrack app. Its Perks & Opportunities feed shows more student discounts on software, tech, travel and food, plus scholarships, internships and hackathons, filtered to your country and interests.
Are these affiliate links?
No. Every link goes straight to the brand's official student offer page. ClassTrack isn't affiliated with these brands. Brands that want to list a student offer can partner with us.
