Perks & Opportunities FAQ

What are Perks & Opportunities in ClassTrack?
A curated feed inside ClassTrack that surfaces student scholarships, internships, hackathons, competitions, grants, free courses and exclusive student discounts, matched to your country and interests. You can bookmark the ones you like and apply in a couple of taps so you never miss a deadline.
How are opportunities matched to me?
ClassTrack uses your country and interests to filter the feed, so you mainly see opportunities you actually qualify for instead of scrolling through awards and internships that don't apply to you.
Is the Perks & Opportunities feed free?
Yes. Browsing scholarships, internships, hackathons, grants and student discounts in ClassTrack is free. Legitimate opportunities never charge an application fee — a request to pay to apply is a red flag.
Which app is this?
This is ClassTrack, the free student app at classtracks.app with the Google Play package name com.classtracks.app. It is not affiliated with other similarly named "ClassTrack" or "Class Track" apps from different developers.
